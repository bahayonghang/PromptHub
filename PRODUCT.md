# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

desktop

## Users

PromptHub is primarily for individual developers and advanced AI users who work
across tools such as Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor. They need a dependable place
to author and refine prompts while building a structured library they can search,
maintain, and reuse over time.

## Product Purpose

PromptHub helps users write, debug, and compare prompts, then preserve the useful
results as an organized local knowledge base. Version history, model comparison,
and rules support that core workflow. Success means the user finishes
with a clear, maintainable prompt library rather than another collection of
scattered text files and chat fragments.

## Positioning

A local-first prompt knowledge base with version evolution and outcome
validation.

## Brand Personality

Professional, powerful, and technical. PromptHub should feel like a capable
desktop tool whose depth becomes apparent through efficient workflows and
precise behavior, not through decorative complexity. Raycast is the primary
experience reference for desktop efficiency, compact interaction, and polished
execution.

## Anti-references

PromptHub must not resemble a generic SaaS dashboard. Avoid oversized cards,
gradients, badge-heavy layouts, decorative metrics, and presentation patterns
that make an operational tool feel like a marketing page.

## Design Principles

1. **The library is the outcome.** Authoring, testing, and comparison should
   continuously improve a durable, searchable body of prompt knowledge.
2. **Desktop efficiency earns trust.** Keep common actions close, predictable,
   and fast; make advanced capability discoverable without slowing routine work.
3. **Local-first must be tangible.** Data ownership, persistence, version
   history, and failure states should be explicit enough that users understand
   what is stored and what is safe.
4. **Power without dashboard theater.** Use density, hierarchy, and precise
   controls to communicate capability instead of decorative cards or metrics.
5. **Verification belongs in the workflow.** Model comparison and version
   evolution should connect directly to the prompt being maintained, not feel
   like detached utilities.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Every workflow must be operable by keyboard, focus must remain
clearly visible, reduced-motion preferences must be respected, and status or
selection must never rely on color alone.
